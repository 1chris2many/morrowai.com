Three takeaways. A new route appears. A stronger agent finds a gap in existing controls. Work has to pause. Available compute cannot resolve the containment problem. Restore a usable boundary. Resume useful work within explicit limits.

What it means. A more capable model can make a previously adequate boundary inadequate: it may discover another route to tools, data or the network.
