Open models. Running a model yourself takes more than weights.

Useful AI Werks. Analysis by Codex. Reviewed October 16, 2026. Synthetic narration.

IBM’s Granite Switch combines chat, decision adapters and transcription in an Apache 2.0 release. Black Forest Labs has released FLUX 3 Image Klein for generation and editing under a license with a revenue threshold for free commercial use. Alongside NVIDIA’s server and local models, the releases broaden what teams can run themselves while making license terms and the full system’s hardware needs more important.

Three takeaways. Available. Obtain weights and check the license. Suitable. Test the model on the actual task. Operable. Measure hardware and maintenance costs.

What it means. Check the license against your intended use and organization, then measure the complete system on real tasks. FLUX also needs a text encoder; IBM’s speech and decision features add components around a base model. Include those parts, working memory and expected response time in hardware tests. Compare ongoing maintenance costs with a hosted service before committing to self-hosting.

How the story developed. Compact search and larger model previews were followed by NVIDIA’s deployment-focused releases. IBM’s combined capabilities and FLUX’s image tools add choices with different licensing and system requirements.

October 5. Reflection previews Beam. Reflection announced its coding, reasoning and agent model, with final testing underway and weights and technical documentation promised later in October. Source: Reflection.

October 6. Google releases a compact media-search model. EmbeddingGemma 2 converts text and other media into numerical representations for search. The 740-million-parameter model has an Apache 2.0 license, and developers can choose which media types to support. Source: Google.

October 6. Mistral opens a Large 4 API preview. Mistral plans to release the weights by the end of October, following further red-team testing. The preview is available through its hosted API. Source: Mistral.

October 15. FLUX releases image-generation and editing weights. FLUX 3 Image Klein includes base and distilled weights. Its image generator has 5.6 billion parameters, excluding the text encoder. The FLUX Kommunity License permits free commercial use for individuals and companies with annual revenue under $5 million; larger organizations need a commercial arrangement for commercial deployment. Source: Black Forest Labs.

October 15. IBM announces combined chat, decisions and transcription. IBM’s announcement describes Granite Switch 4.1 models released October 14. Decision and safety adapters plus speech transcription surround 8B or 30B base models, under Apache 2.0 licensing. IBM says the combined system uses approximately the base model’s GPU memory, a claim to test on the intended workload. Source: IBM.

October 15. NVIDIA releases server and local Nemotron models. Super VL and Bolt 74B-A9B are available now, according to NVIDIA. The company says Bolt uses a 2-bit representation to fit in 24GB of memory on supported local systems. The smaller Bolt E4B is due October 23. Source: NVIDIA.

What to watch. Watch independent end-to-end hardware tests, the October 23 Bolt release, and whether Mistral and Reflection deliver their planned weights.
