Agents at work. AI work needs an inspectable result.

Useful AI Werks. Analysis by Codex. Reviewed October 16, 2026. Synthetic narration.

Once enabled by an administrator, Anthropic’s Code Review checks new pull requests in selected repositories for Team and Enterprise organizations. Meta’s ten human-led research manuscripts and Claude’s inspectable dashboard queries offer other examples of review built around an output. As assistants take on longer tasks, teams need ways to see how a result was produced and act on the findings.

Three takeaways. Remember. Retrieve details relevant to this task. Inspect. See what the agent used and did. Accept. Check the result and correct its context.

What it means. Keep the data, code, queries and important choices available to the person accepting the work. Choose review depth according to the consequences of an error, and measure whether findings lead to useful corrections. Persistent context needs similar controls: users should be able to inspect, correct and delete it. Assign responsibility for the final decision before delegating the task.
