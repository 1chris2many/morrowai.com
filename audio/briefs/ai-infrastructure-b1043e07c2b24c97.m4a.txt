AI infrastructure. Power and hardware for AI

Useful AI Werks. Analysis by Codex. Reviewed October 12, 2026. Synthetic narration.

Microsoft plans multi-gigawatt Maia deployments starting in 2027. NVIDIA’s latest contributions cover racks, cooling interfaces and data-center standards. The announcements show two ways to expand capacity: optimize a system for known workloads, and give suppliers common designs to build and operate together.

Three takeaways. Power. When does electricity arrive?. Capacity. What is running today?. Access. Who can use and afford it?.

What it means. Chip performance is only one input to a working facility. Power availability, cooling, compatible equipment and maintenance can each determine when capacity becomes useful. Treat vendor efficiency figures as testable claims, then compare them on the actual workload. Keep the delivery calendar visible: existing production systems, 2027 chip deployments and power additions expected by 2028 are separate planning commitments.
