Manus — September 28, 2026

Manus 2.0 adds event-triggered work and a separate personal-agent app

Manus announced a rebuilt agent framework, desktop workspace and event-triggered automations on September 28. Its separate Cue app gives agents communication accounts and a wallet, with spending constrained by the user's budget.

Manus reports lower costs in one tested configuration; teams should measure their own workflows before budgeting around that result.

TechCrunch — October 5, 2026

OpenAI plans EU text watermarks

OpenAI will add invisible marks to eligible ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU over the coming weeks. API users worldwide can opt in for selected models; watermarking remains off by default there.

Edits and short passages can weaken detection, so an absent mark cannot establish human authorship.

Reflection AI — October 5, 2026

Reflection previews Beam ahead of an open-weight release

Reflection says Beam matches GLM-5.2 on selected reasoning tests using roughly a quarter to a third of the estimated inference compute. The model is still in final testing, with Apache 2.0 weights planned later in October.

The comparison excludes serving overhead, so actual hosting costs still need measurement.

TechCrunch — October 5, 2026

Researchers trace an apparent agent fleet through map queries

Researchers found parallel agents investigating which entrances people use at locations listed on Amap. Their preliminary report links the infrastructure to Tencent Cloud but does not establish communication between the agents.

Infrastructure clues can help attribute activity without proving who directed it.

About the editor

Hi, I’m Chris.

I enjoy product questions where the answer isn’t obvious yet. What is someone trying to accomplish? Where does the experience break down? What would an improvement look like?

At Amazon, I worked on Alexa Shopping, Skill Flow Builder, and Amazon Rufus with designers, engineers, scientists, and product leaders. Before product management, I served in the U.S. Army for 12 years and earned an MBA at Vanderbilt.
