Anthropic

Anthropic reports changes in junior hiring and agent use

Anthropic’s October 16 study puts employee-directed agent runtime at six times estimated human work hours in August; the human baseline assumes a 40-hour week. The share of junior technical hires fell from 27% to 9% since early 2025, while measured output rose after agent adoption. The company notes that adoption was not randomized.

Review capacity and entry-level hiring deserve separate tracking alongside output measures.

Study published October 16, 2026.

CERN — October 15, 2026

CERN and Mistral plan a model for particle physics

CERN announced on October 15 that it and Mistral signed a statement of intent to develop foundation models for science. Their planned Large Physics Model would train on simulated proton collisions and support tasks including particle identification and detector design.

The proposal would test whether one model can support several stages of collider analysis currently handled by specialized algorithms.

Anthropic Help Center — checked October 18, 2026

Claude Enterprise billing separates access fees from consumption

Anthropic's current Enterprise billing guide describes annual seat fees plus separately metered usage at API rates. Legacy seat arrangements transition at renewal. This is the Enterprise structure, not a description of every Claude subscription.

Customers can retain paid access while changing the amount of work they send to the provider.

DeepSeek — October 17, 2026

DeepSeek updates V4.1 Flash and opens search APIs

DeepSeek released its October 17 V4.1 Flash update across its website, app and API, reporting improvements in coding and agent tasks. It also opened public Search and Fetch APIs, listing each at $0.75 per 1,000 calls; a temporary waiver covers server-side tool calls through supported coding clients.

Model quality and web-tool costs both affect the cost of completing an agent task.

Google Research — October 16, 2026

Google tests AI agents against laboratory reality

Google Research’s October 16 report describes three projects involving laboratory robots and repeated molecular-design experiments. Its VERID system checks proposed robot actions against a live laboratory model before execution, with humans authorizing sequences, checking protocols and supervising runs. Combining the projects into one workflow remains a next step.

Physical-state checks and experimental feedback help expose mistakes that plausible-looking code or molecular designs can miss.

Ramp — October 14, 2026

Ramp finds rising AI token use without matching spending growth

Ramp's October index reports rising token volumes but little token-spending growth in August and September. Anthropic adoption still increased in its business sample, while OpenAI gained faster. Ramp's economist attributes the spending pattern to lower blended prices and efficiency improvements.

Usage, customer adoption and provider revenue can move at different rates.

Ramp — October 6, 2026

Ramp records more businesses switching AI providers

Ramp reports that its three-month-average monthly provider-switching rate reached 8% in September, a record in its dataset. The measure covers businesses observed by Ramp, not the whole AI market.

More frequent switching makes the durability of a provider's customer base a question worth tracking.

Capital on Tap — October 12, 2026

UK small-business AI adoption grows as spending shares shift

Among Capital on Tap's UK business cardholders, AI adoption rose from 12.8% in Q2 to 14.5% in Q3. Claude's share of AI spending slipped from 54.1% to 52.2%, while ChatGPT's rose from 27.3% to 32.4%. The figures cover direct card payments and exclude invoiced contracts.

This sample shows broader adoption alongside a shift in spending share; Claude remained the spending leader.
