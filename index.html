What I Do

What should we build? How will we know it works? What will it take to deliver? I work with teams to answer those questions and turn the answers into a practical plan.

GenAI & LLM Product Strategy

Start with the customer's task, define acceptable performance, and choose how to evaluate it. I help teams connect those decisions to a product roadmap, with explicit tradeoffs around quality, cost, and speed.

Conversational Commerce & Voice AI

Help customers who know what they need to accomplish but don't yet know what to ask. I work on conversation entry points, follow-up questions, and product discovery across text and voice experiences.

Search, Ranking & Recommendation

Understand where discovery breaks down: interpreting the query, retrieving useful candidates, or ranking the results. I help teams identify the problem and measure whether a change improves the customer experience.

Agentic Commerce & AI Agents

Helping someone choose a product and purchasing it on their behalf require different capabilities. I help teams assess customer value, payment authorization, responsibility for mistakes, and merchant integration before committing to a roadmap.

About

I enjoy product questions where the answer isn't obvious yet. My starting point is the customer: what are they trying to accomplish, where does the experience break down, and what would an improvement look like?

At Amazon, I worked on Alexa Shopping, Skill Flow Builder, and Amazon Rufus with designers, engineers, scientists, and product leaders. That work informs how I approach conversational experiences, product discovery, and evaluation.

Before product management, I served in the U.S. Army for 12 years and earned an MBA at Vanderbilt. Those experiences shaped how I lead teams and connect a strategy to the work required to carry it out.

In my independent consulting practice, I help teams understand the problem, test their assumptions, and choose a useful next step. We don't need every answer at the outset. We need a clear way to learn and make decisions.

Speaking & Mentorship

Past speaking includes Product School and GDC 2019. I have also mentored veterans through American Corporate Partners.
